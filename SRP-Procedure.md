- An easy way to measure SRP using a spectrophotometer
# Materials
## PPE
- Lab coat
- Goggles
- Nitrile Gloves
## Chemicals
- Concentrated Sulfuric Acid
- Deionized water
- Potassium Antimony Tartrate
- Ammonium molybdate $\circ$ 4H$_2$O
- Ascorbic acid
# Preparation
**Note:** Phosphate Reagent is stable for *4 hours*. All reagents can be kept at room temperature, except Ascorbic acid which should be stored at 4$^{\circ}$C.
## Working Stock
- Add 0.135077g to a too a 100mL volumetric flask and bring to volume
	- 10mM solution
- Add 0.1 mL of the above 10mM solution to a separate 100mL volumetric flask and bring to volume
	- $10 \mu M$ solution
## 5N Sulfuric Acid
- Mix 70mL conc. and 500mL water
## Potassium Antimony Tartrate
- Mix 0.2742g Potassium Antimony Tartrate with 100mL water
## Ammonium molybdate $\circ$ 4H$_2$O
- Mix 4g Ammonium molybdate $\circ$ 4H$_2$O with 100mL water
## Ascorbic Acid
*Stable one week*
- Mix 1.76g Ascorbic acid with 100ml water
## Combined Reagent
*This is enough for 50 samples and standards*
1. Mix 50mL [[Soluble Reactive Phosphorus#5N Sulfuric Acid|5N Sulfuric acid]] with 5mL [[Soluble Reactive Phosphorus#Potassium Antimony Tartrate|Potassium Antimony Tartrate]]
2. Mix in 15mL [[Soluble Reactive Phosphorus#Ammonium molybdate $ circ$ 4H$_2$O|Ammonium molybdate]]
3. Add 30mL [[Soluble Reactive Phosphorus#Ascorbic Acid|Ascorbic Acid]]

**Ratio:** 1 part potassium antimony tartrate, 3 parts ammonium molybdate 6 parts ascorbic acid, 10 parts sulfuric acid
# Procedure
## Standards
Standards should be prepared as in the table below. Depending on your sample concentrations, you may need a higher standard, or you may not need all of the standards below. Working standard concentration is below, you will have to dilute the 100 mg/L PO4 stock to make this stock when you are ready to run your samples.

|       | **H$_2$O Volume (mL)** | **Working Stock Volume (mL)** | **Target Concentration ($\mu$g L$^{-1}$)** |
| :---: | :--------------------: | :---------------------------: | :----------------------------------------: |
| **1** |           10           |               0               |                     0                      |
| **2** |           10           |             0.10              |                    3.10                    |
| **3** |           10           |             0.20              |                    6.10                    |
| **4** |           10           |             0.40              |                   11.90                    |
| **5** |           10           |             1.00              |                   28.20                    |
| **6** |           10           |             2.00              |                   51.70                    |
| **7** |           10           |             4.00              |                   88.60                    |

## Protocol
1. Pipette 10 ml of DI water into 14 tubes or vials. Add stock in amounts in standard table above.
2. Pipette 10 ml of filtered sample into separate test tubes.
3. Turn on spec.
4. Mix Combined reagent and Ascorbic Acid in correct proportions. Make enough for 2 ml per sample and standard, rounding up to nearest 100 ml to allow for mistakes. When Ascorbic acid is added the solution should turn a light yellow. Sometimes it looks a little yellowish-green
5. Add 2 ml of phosphate reagent to each tube and vortex to mix.
6. Let color develop for at least 10 minutes (20 would be better).
7. Read absorbance on a spectrophotometer at 880 nm.
8. When calculating standards, use both slope and intercept.
